# The Tinikling Method: High-Performance Rhythm Agility Framework

The Tinikling Method is an open-source sports science framework that adapts traditional rhythmic patterns—originating from the Philippine dance Tinikling, alongside West African, Afro-Caribbean, and Hispanic styles—into elite athletic footwork, multi-directional tracking, and team coordination drills for soccer, rugby, tennis, baseball, and swimming.

## 🚀 The Vision: AI-Powered Biomechanical Analytics
While this repository currently hosts structural documentation and foundational math scoring engines, our upcoming roadmap incorporates **Computer Vision Edge AI** architectures designed for the NVIDIA Inception Program. 

By leveraging **NVIDIA TensorRT** and **DeepStream pipelines**, the system will analyze standard high-frame-rate video to track human ankle and joint keypoints relative to moving boundaries. It calculates critical performance vectors in real-time:
* **Ground Contact Time (GCT):** Velocity of foot strikes inside and outside the rhythm zone.
* **Cognitive Load Latency:** Measuring changes in cadence when external sport-specific elements (balls, passes, stroke changes) are introduced.
* **Interpersonal Sync Index:** Phase-alignment algorithms tracking synchronization between multi-athlete defensive or attacking units.
* **Rotational Uncoiling Latency:** Measuring kinetic separation between hips and hands for bat and racket sports.
