# Official Rulebook: National Collegiate Rhythm Agility Tournament

This document defines the competitive format, technical regulations, and scoring matrices for the National Collegiate Rhythm Agility Tournament.

## 1. Tournament Structure & Divisions
The tournament features three distinct tracking brackets open to NCAA, NAIA, and collegiate club programs:
* **Field Athletics Division:** Optimized for Soccer, Rugby, and Football programs.
* **Court & Diamond Division:** Optimized for Tennis, Volleyball, and Baseball programs.
* **Aquatic Precision Division:** Optimized for Competitive Swimming and Diving programs.

## 2. Competitive Grid Specifications
1. **The Grid Zone:** Drills must be performed within a bounded zone measuring exactly 3 meters by 3 meters.
2. **The Rhythmic Input:** Double training poles or submerged acoustic arrays must maintain a sustained pace between **110 BPM and 140 BPM**, depending on the drill seed.
3. **Personnel:** Each active run requires exactly two (2) Competing Athletes and four (4) Rhythmic Actuators (Pole-Clappers/Acoustic Monitors).

## 3. Official Scoring Matrix & Deductions

An execution score begins at a perfect **100.00 Base Points** and is parsed through computer vision tracking:

### A. Performance Bonuses
* **Elite Quick-Twitch Windows:** +5.00 points if the team average Ground Contact Time (GCT) stays consistently below **140 milliseconds**.
* **Flawless Phase Alignment:** +10.00 points if the *Interpersonal Sync Index* sustains a value above **0.9500** throughout a 60-second freestyle sequence.

### B. Regulatory Point Deductions
* **Boundary Infringement (Pole Catch):** -2.50 points every single time a moving pole makes physical contact with an athlete's foot, ankle, or uniform out of cadence.
* **Heads-Down Fault:** -1.50 points per occurrence if an athlete's tracking vector reveals they looked down at their feet for more than 1.5 consecutive seconds.
* **Cadence Disconnect:** -3.00 points if an athlete skips a systematic beat or drops below the required multi-cultural polyrhythm matrix.

## 4. Hardware and Verification Compliance
All matches must be adjudicated using calibrated camera arrays running at a minimum of **60fps (120fps for Aquatics)**. Video feeds must connect directly to the approved version of the *RhythmAgilityEngine* parsing backend. Final analytics profiles are certified and locked into the tournament database.
